About Me

I am a mathematician currently working as a Project JRF (DRDO) at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research, Bhopal. My work sits at the intersection of pure mathematics and its applications in science and defence research.

I completed my Masters in 2023 from the Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science (IACS), School of Mathematical and Computational Sciences, with Mathematics as my major.

Mathematics, to me, is the art of reasoning in its purest form — a language that describes structure, symmetry, and the deep patterns hidden in abstraction. I am drawn to problems where rigour meets creativity.
